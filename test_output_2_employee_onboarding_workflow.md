# New Employee Onboarding Workflow

## Introduction

Effective onboarding is crucial for new hires to quickly adapt to their roles, become productive, and contribute to the organization's success. A well-structured onboarding workflow ensures that HR and IT teams work together seamlessly to provide a smooth transition for new employees. This article outlines the comprehensive onboarding workflow for new hires, including account setup, policy training, system access, and verification.

## Background

The onboarding process typically begins before the new employee's first day, with HR and IT teams collaborating to prepare necessary documents, systems, and access. Onboarding involves several stages, including:

1. Pre-onboarding: Before the new employee's first day, HR and IT teams prepare necessary documents, systems, and access.
2. Day 1: The new employee receives an overview of the organization, meets their team, and begins policy training.
3. Week 1-2: The new employee completes policy training, receives system access, and begins working on assigned tasks.
4. Ongoing: HR and IT teams monitor the new employee's progress, provide support, and address any issues that arise.

## Prerequisites

Before implementing the onboarding workflow, ensure that the following prerequisites are met:

1. **Active Directory (AD)**: Ensure that the organization's AD is up-to-date and configured correctly.
2. **Workday**: Ensure that the organization's Workday system is configured correctly and accessible to HR and IT teams.
3. **System Access**: Ensure that the necessary systems and applications are accessible to new employees.
4. **Policy Training**: Ensure that policy training is available and accessible to new employees.
5. **HR and IT Collaboration**: Ensure that HR and IT teams are collaborating effectively to ensure a smooth onboarding process.

# Detailed Implementation Steps

The following steps outline the detailed implementation of the onboarding workflow:

1. **Pre-Onboarding**:
	* HR teams create a new employee account in Workday.
	* IT teams create a new user account in Active Directory.
	* HR and IT teams collaborate to prepare necessary documents and systems.
2. **Day 1**:
	* HR teams provide an overview of the organization and its policies.
	* IT teams provide system access and training on necessary systems and applications.
	* HR and IT teams monitor the new employee's progress and address any issues that arise.
3. **Week 1-2**:
	* HR teams provide policy training and ensure that the new employee understands organizational policies.
	* IT teams monitor system access and ensure that the new employee has the necessary access to complete assigned tasks.
4. **Ongoing**:
	* HR and IT teams monitor the new employee's progress and provide support as needed.
	* HR teams conduct regular check-ins to ensure that the new employee is adapting to their role.

## Multiple Examples

The following examples illustrate the onboarding workflow in different scenarios:

1. **New Hire with Existing System Access**: If a new hire has existing system access, HR and IT teams can expedite the onboarding process by focusing on policy training and system access.
2. **New Hire without System Access**: If a new hire does not have existing system access, HR and IT teams must create a new user account in Active Directory and provide system access.
3. **Remote Onboarding**: If a new hire is remote, HR and IT teams can use video conferencing tools to provide an overview of the organization and its policies, and to provide system access and training.

# Common Issues & Troubleshooting

The following common issues and troubleshooting steps can help resolve issues that arise during the onboarding process:

1. **System Access Issues**: If a new employee experiences system access issues, IT teams can troubleshoot by checking user account settings, system access permissions, and network connectivity.
2. **Policy Training Issues**: If a new employee experiences policy training issues, HR teams can troubleshoot by checking policy training materials, ensuring that the new employee understands organizational policies, and providing additional training as needed.
3. **Communication Breakdowns**: If there are communication breakdowns between HR and IT teams, they can troubleshoot by establishing clear communication channels, ensuring that both teams are aware of the onboarding process, and providing regular updates.

## Best Practices

The following best practices can help ensure a smooth onboarding process:

1. **Clear Communication**: Ensure that HR and IT teams communicate clearly and effectively to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
2. **Regular Check-Ins**: Conduct regular check-ins with new employees to ensure that they are adapting to their role and to address any issues that arise.
3. **Policy Training**: Provide policy training to new employees to ensure that they understand organizational policies and procedures.
4. **System Access**: Ensure that new employees have the necessary system access to complete assigned tasks.
